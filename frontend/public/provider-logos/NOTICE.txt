Provider logos in this directory are the official vector marks served by
https://models.dev/logos/ (models.dev catalog) and
https://openrouter.ai/brand/ (OpenRouter brand page), with brand colours baked
in by scripts/fetch-provider-logos.ts.

Exceptions — marks sourced from the LobeHub icon project (MIT), used where the
catalog glyph is not the provider's own brand mark:

  alibaba-token-plan.svg, alibaba-token-plan-cn.svg
    百炼 / Bailian (Alibaba Cloud Model Studio) mark
    Source: https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons
    License: MIT

  volcengine.svg, volcengine-coding-plan.svg
    火山引擎 / Volcengine three-peak mark
    Source: https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons
    License: MIT
